# Third-party material

DisinfoMM indexes fact checks from Snopes, Pagella Politica, and Polígrafo and may link to evidence from other publishers. Those organizations are not affiliated with or endorsing this repository.

The GitHub repository contains no benchmark media. The dataset release stores URLs and research annotations; optional download tooling retrieves media directly from the recorded source URLs. Any downloaded media retains its original rights and terms. Do not redistribute a media bundle unless you have independently established permission.

Model implementations rely on separately installed packages and downloaded checkpoints, including OpenAI CLIP/OpenCLIP, PyTorch, Transformers, and M-CLIP. Their own licenses apply.

NewsCLIPpings and FACTIFY are comparison datasets and are not redistributed here. Obtain them from their official releases and comply with their terms.
